One emoji for a dream vacation: **🛫** — 1 character (U+1F6EB, airplane departure), one codepoint, 4 bytes UTF-8. The moment of leaving: wheels up, the trip already happening. It is the flight the judge asks a stranger to want to book — a plane is the brief's own example of a dream-vacation emoji, vivid at a glance, specific to the feeling of departure rather than "a holiday", and a fresher pick than the island everyone submits.
